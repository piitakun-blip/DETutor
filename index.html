Accusative-Only:

Dative-Only:

aus, bei, mit, nach, seit, von, zu

💡 Easy German Gender Shortcut:

In German, all nouns have genders (masculine 🔵, feminine 🔴, or neuter 🟢). When nouns become plural, they all use **the same articles and pronouns as feminine nouns** in the Nominative and Accusative (e.g., *die* Kinder), but require specific plural-ending changes in the Dative (*den* Kindern).
